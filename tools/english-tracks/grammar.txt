## Simple present
Saleh ..... a science teacher. He works at Kingdom School in Riyadh. || is | are | am | be || Saleh مفرد (He) ← نستخدم is.
Look at this cake my sister made. ..... the best baker in our family. || She's | She'is | Shes' | She-is || She's اختصار She is، والفاصلة العلوية مكان حرف i المحذوف (She + 's).
He ..... much in class, but he gets A+ for the essays he writes. || doesn't talk | doesn't | not talk | don't talk || He ← النفي doesn't + الفعل الأساسي talk (بدون s).
A: ..... to go tonight? B: Let's go to an art gallery first and then have dinner at Le Chateau. || Where do you want | Where do you wanting | Where does you wanting | Where does you want || you ← WH + do + Subject + الفعل الأساسي: Where do you want…?

These cookies ..... delicious. Can I have another one? || are | is | am | be || These cookies جمع (They) ← نستخدم are.

We ..... TV in the evening. We prefer to read books or play games. || don't watch | doesn't watch | don't watches | not watch || We ← النفي don't + الفعل الأساسي watch.
A: What time ..... the bank open? B: At 9:30 in the morning. || does | do | is | are || the bank = It ← WH + does + Subject + verb (open هنا فعل).
My little brother ..... every time he can't find his toys. Then my mother helps him look. || cries | crys | cry | crying || He ← قبل y حرف غير متحرك (r) فنحذف y ونضيف ies: cries.

A: ..... you a new student here? B: Yes, I am. I started yesterday. || Are | Is | Do | Am || you ← سؤال Yes/No بفعل be: Are you…? والجواب Yes, I am.

## Simple past
I ..... to my brother's football match, so he was angry with me. || didn't go | gone | not go | doesn't go || أخوه زعل (was angry) يعني الكلام عن الماضي ← النفي: didn't + الفعل الأساسي go.
In the 1900s, people ..... wood to make fires to stay warm. || burned | burns | burning | burn || «In the 1900s» زمن ماضي ← burn + ed = burned.
I was very tired yesterday, so I ..... to bed at nine o'clock. || went | go | goes | going || «yesterday» ماضي ← go ماضيه went (فعل شاذ).
A: ..... you at home last night? B: No, I was at my cousin's house. || Were | Was | Did | Are || مع you نستخدم were ← Were you…? والجواب I was.
My grandfather ..... a teacher when he was young. He taught math at a school in Taif. || was | were | is | did || «when he was young» و«taught» ماضي، و My grandfather = He ← مع He / She / It / I نستخدم was.
The students ..... very happy after the exam. It was easy. || were | was | are | did || The students جمع (They) ← في الماضي were.
We ..... at the mall yesterday. We stayed at home all day. || weren't | wasn't | didn't | don't || We ← النفي were + not = weren't (لأنهم جلسوا في البيت).
A: Did you watch the match last night? B: No, I ...... || didn't | don't | wasn't | did || Did you…? ← الجواب المختصر: No, I didn't.
My sister ..... a beautiful dress for her friend's wedding last week. It was very expensive. || bought | buyed | buys | buy || «last week» ماضي ← buy فعل شاذ، ماضيه bought.
They ..... to Jeddah by plane. The flight took one hour and a half. || flew | flied | fly | flying || الرحلة خلصت (took) ← fly فعل شاذ، ماضيه flew.
A: What ..... you eat for breakfast this morning? B: Eggs and bread. || did | do | does | were || السؤال في الماضي: What + did + you + الفعل الأساسي (eat).
I ..... my homework last night because I was sick, so my teacher gave me one more day. || didn't do | didn't did | don't do | not did || «last night» ماضي ← didn't + الفعل الأساسي do (مو did).
Khalid ..... his keys this morning, so he couldn't open the door. || lost | losed | loses | lose || «this morning» صار وخلص ← lose فعل شاذ، ماضيه lost.
My father ..... me a new phone for my birthday last month. || gave | gived | gives | give || «last month» ماضي ← give فعل شاذ، ماضيه gave.
Our baby ..... for hours last night, so my parents didn't get any sleep. || cried | cryed | cries | crys || «last night» ماضي، وقبل y حرف غير متحرك (r) ← نحذف y ونضيف ied: cry ← cried.
We ..... a great time at the beach last weekend. We swam and played football all day. || had | haved | have | has || «last weekend» ماضي ← have فعل شاذ، ماضيه had.
A: Where ..... you go on your holiday last year? B: We went to Malaysia. || did | do | were | was || سؤال ماضي فيه فعل (go) ← Where + did + you + الفعل الأساسي.
I ..... Sara at the supermarket two days ago. She said hello to me. || met | meeted | meet | meets || «two days ago» ماضي ← meet فعل شاذ، ماضيه met.
The film ..... very long. It was almost three hours! || was | were | is | did || The film = It ← في الماضي was.
Omar ..... to study for the math exam last week, so he failed it. || didn't try | didn't tried | doesn't try | not tried || «last week» ماضي والنتيجة صارت (failed) ← didn't + الفعل الأساسي try.
My mother ..... a delicious cake for my brother's party yesterday. Everyone loved it. || made | maked | makes | make || «yesterday» ماضي ← make فعل شاذ، ماضيه made.
A: Was the test difficult? B: No, it ...... It was easy. || wasn't | weren't | didn't | isn't || Was it…? ← الجواب المختصر: No, it wasn't.
We ..... the bus because we were late. We took a taxi instead. || missed | missd | miss | missing || الكلام عن موقف صار (took a taxi) ← miss + ed = missed.
I ..... the news on TV last night. It was about the weather. || saw | seen | see | sees || «last night» ماضي ← see فعل شاذ، ماضيه saw.
A: ..... your brother call you yesterday? B: Yes, he called me in the evening. || Did | Was | Does | Is || «yesterday» ماضي وفيه فعل (call) ← Did + your brother + الفعل الأساسي.

## Should / Shouldn't
You ..... cigarettes. They are very bad for your health and for the people around you. || shouldn't smoke | should smoking | should smoke | shouldn't smoking || التدخين يضر (الجملة الثانية توضح) ← shouldn't + الفعل الأساسي (smoke)، بدون ing.
You ..... the garbage out once it's full to avoid bad smells. || should throw | should | shouldn't | shouldn't throw || رمي الزبالة شي زين عشان ما تطلع ريحة ← should + الفعل الأساسي throw.
You look very tired. You ..... go to bed early tonight. || should | shouldn't | should to | shoulds || النوم بدري شي زين للتعبان ← should + go.
You ..... drink coffee before bed. It will keep you awake. || shouldn't | should | should not to | don't should || القهوة قبل النوم تسهّرك (شي مو زين) ← shouldn't + drink.
Students ..... use their phones during the exam. The teacher will take them if they do. || shouldn't | should | shouldn't to | doesn't should || الجوال وقت الاختبار ممنوع والمعلم بياخذه ← shouldn't + use (بدون to).
It's very cold outside. You ..... wear a jacket. || should | shouldn't | should to | are should || البرد يحتاج جاكيت (شي زين) ← should + wear.
A: I have a terrible toothache. B: You ..... see a dentist. || should | shouldn't | should to | should seeing || النصيحة للي عنده ألم أسنان: should + see.
A: ..... I take an umbrella? B: Yes, you should. It's going to rain. || Should | Do | Am | Shoulds || السؤال: Should + I + الفعل الأساسي، والجواب Yes, you should.
A: Should we eat fast food every day? B: No, we ...... || shouldn't | should | don't should | aren't || الأكل السريع كل يوم مو زين ← No, we shouldn't.
You ..... eat a lot of fruit and vegetables to stay healthy. || should | shouldn't | should to | should eating || الفواكه والخضار شي زين ← should + eat.
My brother drives too fast. He ..... drive more slowly. || should | shouldn't | should to | shoulds || النصيحة الصح يسوق على مهل (شي زين) ← should + drive.
You ..... leave your bag alone at the airport. Someone could easily steal it. || shouldn't | should | should to | shouldn't to || ترك الشنطة لحالها خطر لأن ممكن تنسرق ← shouldn't + leave (بدون to).
What ..... I do? I lost my passport! || should | shoulds | do should | am should || السؤال: What + should + I + الفعل الأساسي (do).
Children ..... play with fire. It's very dangerous. || shouldn't | should | shouldn't to | don't should || اللعب بالنار خطير (شي مو زين) ← shouldn't + play.
If you want to pass the exam, you ..... study every day. || should | shouldn't | should to | should studying || المذاكرة كل يوم شي زين ← should + study.
A: My English is not very good. B: You ..... practice speaking with your friends. || should | shouldn't | should to | should practicing || النصيحة: should + practice (الفعل الأساسي).
You ..... be late for your job interview. The manager will think you are not serious. || shouldn't | should | shouldn't to | don't should || التأخير يعطي صورة سيئة عنك ← shouldn't + be (وما نقول don't should).
We ..... help our parents at home. They work hard and they need our help. || should | shouldn't | should to | should helping || مساعدة الوالدين شي زين ← should + help (بدون to وبدون ing).
A: Should Sara buy that expensive bag? B: No, she ...... She doesn't have enough money. || shouldn't | should | doesn't | isn't || ما عندها فلوس كفاية ← No, she shouldn't.
You ..... wash your hands before you eat. They carry germs that can make you sick. || should | shouldn't | should to | should washing || غسل اليدين قبل الأكل يحميك من الجراثيم ← should + wash.
People ..... throw rubbish in the street. They should use the bins. || shouldn't | should | should to | don't should || رمي الزبالة في الشارع شي غلط ← shouldn't + throw.
A: Where should we go for dinner? B: We ..... try the new Italian restaurant. || should | shouldn't | should to | shoulds || اقتراح شي زين ← should + try.
He ..... spend all his money on video games. He needs to save some. || shouldn't | should | shouldn't to | doesn't should || صرف كل فلوسه على الألعاب شي مو زين ← shouldn't + spend.
You ..... drink a lot of water in summer. It's very hot, and your body needs it. || should | shouldn't | should to | should drinking || الجو حار والجسم يحتاج ماء ← should + drink.
A: Should I tell my parents the truth? B: Yes, you ...... || should | do | are | shoulds || الجواب المختصر: Yes, you should.

## It's + adjective + infinitive
It's ..... new languages if you practice every day. || easy to learn | learn easy | easy learn | easy learn to || الترتيب: It's + صفة (easy) + to + الفعل (learn).

It's ..... to wear a seat belt in the car. It can save your life in an accident. || important | importantly | important not | to important || It's + صفة (important) + to + الفعل. importantly ظرف مو صفة.

It's ..... to swim here. The sea is very rough today. || not safe | safe not | not safe to | safely || النفي: It's + not + صفة + to. والبحر هايج ← It's not safe to swim.
A: Why do you study every day? B: Because it's necessary ..... good grades. || to get | get | getting | to getting || بعد الصفة necessary يجي to + الفعل الأساسي.
It's hard ..... up early in winter. The mornings are cold and dark, and I feel lazy. || to wake | wake | waking to | to waking || It's + hard + to + الفعل الأساسي (wake).

It's polite ..... "please" and "thank you" when you ask for something. People will respect you. || to say | say | says | to saying || It's + polite + to + الفعل الأساسي: to say.

## Be going to
