# أسئلة القواعد — مقسمة حسب قواعد ملزمة المستخدم (store/english/grammar-malzama.pdf)
# الصيغة:  ## اسم القاعدة   ثم:  السؤال || الصحيح | خطأ | خطأ | خطأ || الشرح (يطلع للطالب بعد ما يجاوب، صح أو غلط)
# في كل قاعدة: تدريبات الملزمة + نماذج المستخدم (صور التليجرام) + أسئلة إضافية

## Adjectives + Adverbs (a / an)
It's ..... class. || a pretty interesting | pretty interesting | pretty a interesting | an pretty interesting || اسم مفرد (class) يبدأ بحرف ساكن ← a + Adverb + Adjective + Noun: a pretty interesting class.
They are ...... || beautiful classes | a beautiful classes | an beautiful classes | classes beautiful || classes جمع ← ما نحط a / an، والصفة قبل الاسم: beautiful classes.
I have ...... || smart students | a smart students | an smart students | smarts students || students جمع ← بدون a / an، والصفة ما تنجمع: smart students.
It is ...... || a very safe school | very safe school | very a safe school | an very safe school || school اسم مفرد ← a + very + safe + school.
This is ...... || an extremely quiet classroom | a extremely quiet classroom | extremely quiet classroom | extremely an quiet classroom || تبدأ بـ extremely (حرف علة e) ← an extremely quiet classroom.
The ...... || building is really beautiful | building is a really beautiful | is building really beautiful | really building is beautiful || Subject + Verb be + Adverb + Adjective: The building is really beautiful (بدون a لأنه ما فيه اسم بعد الصفة).
That is ..... idea! I've heard it's not safe to go there after dark. || a really bad | an extremely | a very | a bad really || اسم مفرد (idea) ← a + Adverb + Adjective + Noun، وأول كلمة really حرف ساكن ← a really bad.
My teacher is kind and ...... If I don't understand my math lesson, she explains it to me again. || helpful | a helpful | a helpfuls | helpfuls || بعد Verb be صفة بدون اسم ← ما نحط a: kind and helpful. والصفة ما تنجمع.

The weather today is ...... || extremely hot | an extremely hot | hot extremely | a extremely hot || صفة بعد Verb be بدون اسم ← بدون a: extremely hot.
