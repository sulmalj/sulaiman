# دفعة 4 من المستخدم (2026-10-25) — قطعة جاءت في اختبار (تسريب) — للموقعين. النص كما أرسله (الأسئلة توصل بعدين).
1. What kind of people are your family? What do they look like? As for me, everyone in my family is a bit different. My dad and his brothers, for example, are all quite large. My mom and my sisters are rather thin, though. Actually, everyone on my mom's side of the family is slender. And it's not just how we look. Some people in my family are always shy around new people. They're cool people, but they don't know what to say. Others are very outgoing and love telling stories. It's not a problem though. Often the more outgoing family members introduce shy family members to new friends.
2. It's interesting to think how we are different from each other. There are, however, two things that are true for everyone in my family. First, everyone is very neat. Even busy family members still find time to keep things clean. My cousin, for example, goes to school and has a part-time job at a bookstore. Her life is hectic but her kitchen is always clean because she washes all the dishes and wipes down the tables and counters after each meal.
3. The second thing that is true for everyone in my family is that we're all so nice. We all love to help out our friends and family members. Especially when someone has a big problem, we all focus on helping to solve the problem. For example, recently my uncle lost his job. He is my dad's younger brother. Right away, everyone started texting him to ask how they could help. We also told him about new job opportunities. It wasn't long before he got a new job.
