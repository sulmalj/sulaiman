In most countries, people eat apples all the time. They're sweet, crunchy, and healthy. For this reason, they're a beloved snack for both children and adults. People also love to use apples to make apple juice, apple pie, apple butter, and so on. But while apples are popular everywhere, the people who love apples most might live in Washington State, USA.
Located next to the Pacific Ocean in the northwest of the United States, Washington State is famous for its biggest city, Seattle, and for its technology companies, like Amazon. But it is also a great place to grow apples. The weather is usually sunny, but not too hot, in the summer. This is good because apple trees need at least six hours of sun every day when they're growing in the summer. The weather should be warm, but not too hot outside. However, apple trees also need the temperature to be cold sometimes. Every year, the trees should get between 500 and 1,000 hours between 0 and 5°C. Their flowers don't grow well if the weather isn't cold sometimes. That's fine because in Washington the winter temperatures are a little cold and it can rain 15 to 25 days each month. This would cripple another fruit like an orange, but not apples. In Washington State, farmers grow more than 10 billion (that's 10,000,000,000) of them every year.
The people in Washington love to celebrate their fruit. Starting in the spring, there are Apple Blossom Festivals, where you can listen to music and enjoy the beautiful apple trees. Then, usually in October, there are festivals where you can do things like pick apples, try to win apple-eating contests, and drive around the farms. Many people in Washington think it's the best time of the year.
